# Strawberry and Melon Hand

The second hired hand works the six-cell rectangle directly above the carrot
farmer's field. It harvests ripe strawberries and melons first, waters plants
that need care, and plants only when a crop has a profitable expected mature
yield and seeds are available.

Profitability rule: current market price multiplied by maximum crop yield must
be at least twice the seed cost. Strawberry and melon sales follow the same
rule; stored produce is liquidated from day 27 onward. The hand drops harvested
produce at the shed, while the main agent sends the market sell orders.

When both crops are profitable, the six cells are split evenly between them;
otherwise, the profitable crop uses the available planting slots. The first
hired hand remains the weed keeper. The strawberry/melon hand is the second
hired hand and is hired when planting is profitable or its existing crops need
watering or harvesting.
